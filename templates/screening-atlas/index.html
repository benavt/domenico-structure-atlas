Methods, provenance & limits

Input coordinates and energies come from the completed, hash-accepted 3 November snapshot. This site packages saved results; it does not run clustering, recompute energies or infer new structures. ProteinEBM Model6 expert16 supplies 16 energy levels; the chart shows t = 0.10.

Representatives in the forthcoming report will use genuine native confidence where available and structural medoids for unscored clusters. ProteinEBM energy will remain a separate measurement. Full clustering and molecular exports retain memory caps, zero job swap and sustained-pressure stopping.
